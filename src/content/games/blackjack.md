## How it works

You sit down with 100 virtual chips. No real money is involved anywhere in this game. Each hand starts when you choose a bet and press Deal.

You get two cards face up. The dealer gets two as well, but only the first one is visible. Number cards count as their number, picture cards count as ten, and an ace counts as eleven until that would take you past 21, at which point it counts as one. Get closer to 21 than the dealer and you win the bet. Go past 21 and you lose it straight away.

## Winning the night

The round ends when your stack reaches 500 chips or when you no longer have the five chips needed for a minimum bet. Reaching 500 is the win. Running dry is the loss. The panel at the top tracks your stack, your best stack and the hands played.

A winning hand pays even money, so a bet of 20 comes back as 40. A blackjack, which is an ace with a ten-value card on the first two cards, pays three to two and is rounded down to a whole chip. If you and the dealer end with the same total, the hand is a push and your bet comes back untouched.

## Choosing a bet

Small bets keep you at the table longer but take many hands to reach the target. Large bets get you there faster and can empty your stack in one bad run.

Double down gives you one extra card for twice the original bet, and then your hand is finished. It works best on a total of ten or eleven, where a single ten-value card leaves you near 21. The dealer always draws until reaching 17 or more, so a weak dealer card often means waiting rather than risking a bust yourself.

## Controls

Press H to hit, S to stand and D to double. Use the arrow keys to change your bet and Enter to deal or move to the next hand. Every action also has a button you can click or tap.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is any real money involved?**
No. The chips exist only in your browser for the length of the session, and nothing is stored, paid or collected.

**When can I double down?**
Only on your first two cards, and only when your remaining stack covers a second bet of the same size. The button is greyed out when either condition is missing.

**What does the dealer do after I stand?**
The dealer turns over the hidden card and keeps drawing until the hand reaches 17 or more, then stops. If that takes the dealer past 21, every hand still standing wins.

**Why does my total sometimes say soft?**
A soft total means an ace is currently counting as eleven. You cannot bust on the next card from a soft total, because the ace drops to one instead.
