## How it works

A short passage appears at the top of the screen. Your job is to copy it exactly, including the punctuation and the capital letters. The clock does not start until you press your first key, so you can read the passage first if you want to.

Every character you type is checked against the passage straight away. Correct characters turn solid, and wrong ones are marked in red so you can see the mistake without looking away from the text. A thin line marks the character you are expected to type next. You have sixty seconds. Finish the whole passage inside that time and the round counts as a clear. If the clock runs out first, the round ends and your result is measured from what you managed to type.

## How speed and accuracy are scored

Your speed is reported in words per minute. A word counts as five correct characters, which is the usual way typing speed is measured, so short words and long words are treated fairly. Only characters that match the passage count towards your speed.

Accuracy is separate. It compares the number of keys you pressed against the number of those presses that landed on the right character. Fixing a mistake with backspace does not undo the error in that figure, so the cleanest way to a high score is to slow down slightly rather than to correct constantly. A run has to reach eighty percent accuracy before it can become your personal best. That best is kept on your own device and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Tips for a faster run

Start slower than you think you should. Speed on a typing test comes from a steady rhythm, not from bursts, and every mistake costs you more time to fix than it saved you to rush. Keep your eyes on the passage rather than on your hands.

Watch the next-character marker instead of the whole line. It keeps your focus narrow and stops you from reading ahead and losing your place. If you notice yourself tensing up, let the clock run out on that round and start a fresh one. The passage changes each time, so you are never just memorising the same words.

## Controls

Type on your keyboard. Backspace fixes the last character. The buttons below the passage give you a new passage or let you quit back to the start screen.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the clock start as soon as the page loads?**
No. It starts on your first keystroke, so you can read the passage before you begin. Once it starts it runs for sixty seconds without pausing.

**Why is my accuracy lower than it looks on screen?**
The passage shows the state of the text as it stands now, after your corrections. Accuracy counts every key you pressed, including the ones you went back and fixed.

**Can I use my phone?**
Yes. The passage and the input box both work on a touch keyboard, though a physical keyboard will give you a much higher reading.

**Is my personal best shared with anyone?**
No. It is stored only in your own browser, and clearing your site data removes it.
