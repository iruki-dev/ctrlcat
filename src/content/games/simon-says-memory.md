## How it works

The board holds four pads: violet, pink, sky and amber. A round starts with the board lighting one pad at a time while you watch. When the flashing stops, your turn begins and you press the same pads in the same order.

Clear a round and the board replays the whole sequence with one new flash on the end. The pads also light up a little faster as the sequence grows, so you get less time to recall each step. Clear twenty rounds and you win.

## Where it gets hard

The first six or seven rounds feel easy because you can hold the colours in your head as a short list. Past that point the list is too long to rehearse one item at a time. Most players switch to chunking: they group the flashes into pairs or triples and remember a handful of small patterns instead of one long chain.

Rhythm helps as much as colour does. The pads flash at a steady beat, so the sequence has a shape you can hum back to yourself. If you lose the beat, you usually lose the order too.

## Tips and scoring

Your score is the number of rounds you clear, and the best score on the panel keeps the highest run of your session. One wrong pad ends the game straight away, so accuracy beats speed. Take the pause before you answer; nothing is timing you during your turn.

Watch the whole sequence before you move your hand. Players who start answering while the board is still flashing tend to lose the tail end of the order. If you blank halfway through, pick the pad that fits the rhythm rather than freezing, because a guess at least keeps the run alive.

## Controls

Click or tap a pad to press it. On a keyboard, press 1, 2, 3 or 4 for the pads in reading order, or use Q and W for the top row and A and S for the bottom row.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the sequence change every round?**
No. The same sequence is replayed from the start each round with one new flash added at the end. That is why the early steps become automatic while the last few stay fragile.

**What happens if I press the wrong pad?**
The round ends immediately and the pad turns red. Your score stays at the number of rounds you already cleared, and you can start a fresh run from the result screen.

**Can I pause during my turn?**
There is no timer on your turn, so you can take as long as you like between presses. Use the Quit button if you want to stop a run and return to the start screen.
