## What this is

Lorem ipsum is filler text that has been used in printing and design for centuries. It looks like language but carries no meaning, so nobody reads it instead of looking at the layout. This tool builds that filler text for you in the amount you ask for.

You choose whether to count paragraphs, sentences or words, set how many you want, and the text appears right away. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing you generate is sent anywhere.

## Where this is useful

Filling a page mockup so a client can judge the spacing and type size before the real copy exists.

Testing how a card, table cell or heading behaves when the text is much longer than the sample you had in mind.

Seeding a database or a content model with rows that have realistic-looking body text.

Checking that a text field, comment box or preview card truncates and wraps the way you expect.

Preparing a print layout where the wording has not been approved yet but the design review is today.

## How to use this tool

Pick a unit: paragraphs, sentences or words. Set the amount you need in the count box. Leave the opening line option on if you want the text to begin with the familiar "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet" phrase, or turn it off for a different start every time.

Press Generate. The result appears below with a word and character count. Press Copy to put it on your clipboard, or Generate again for a fresh version. Clear resets the output.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the text always the same?**
No. The words are drawn at random from a classic Latin word list, so each run gives you something different. Only the opening line stays fixed, and that is optional.

**How much text can I make at once?**
You can ask for up to 200 units. That is enough for a long article mockup, and it keeps the result quick to generate and easy to copy.

**Does the text mean anything?**
Not in any useful sense. The words come from Latin, but the order is scrambled, so it reads as texture rather than content. That is the point: reviewers look at the design instead of the wording.

**Is my generated text stored anywhere?**
No. The text is created in your browser and disappears when you close or reload the page.
